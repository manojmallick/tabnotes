Sticky notes on web pages

Add sticky notes on web pages without losing them when the browser gets busy.

TabNotes lets you leave notes directly on a real page, then decide whether they should live only for this tab, come back on the same URL, follow the whole site, or stay available everywhere.

Chrome sticky notes See pricing

Typical failure: many tools let you place a note on a page once, but they do not help with revisit behavior, search, or turning those notes into an organized workflow.

TabNotes approach: page-level stickies plus persistence scopes, dashboard search, exports, reminders, and source-linked captures.

What “on-page” should mean

A web-page sticky note should feel native to the browsing flow, not like a detached widget.

Fast capture

Create a note from the popup, floating dock, context menu, selection highlight, side panel, or keyboard shortcut.

Page-aware persistence

Choose whether the note belongs to a tab, an exact page, the current site, or a broader browser project.

Source-linked notes

Turn selections, highlighted text, screenshots, images, and OCR into notes that still point back to the page they came from.

Recoverable later

Search, filter, archive, export, and re-open notes from the dashboard once they stop being “quick notes” and become part of work.
