Support

Get help with TabNotes.

Use this page as the public support/contact URL for the Chrome Web Store listing, dashboard launch audit, and in-product trust flow.

Contact support

For launch questions, bug reports, or billing follow-up, email hello@tabnotes.app.

The site launch config can replace this address before publication.

Email support

Before you reach out

  • Reload the extension in chrome://extensions after updating.
  • Confirm the page is a normal website, not a blocked or unsupported Chrome surface.
  • Check Dashboard - Launch for dependency or release-audit warnings.
  • Export a JSON backup before replacing your workspace or testing imports.

Recommended bug report details

  • The page URL or domain where the issue happened.
  • Whether the note used tab, URL, domain, global, text, or element scope.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or exported share bundle if the issue is visual or workflow-related.

Trust and privacy

TabNotes is local-first by default. Review the privacy policy for storage, sync, screenshot, OCR, AI, and licensing details before public launch.

If you are testing launch flows, keep the dashboard support URL and privacy-policy URL aligned with the live pages you plan to publish.
