Why this packaging

TabNotes should win by becoming part of the work, not by locking basic notes behind paywalls.

Free users still get a functional sticky-note extension. Pro turns that into a contextual browser workflow system with persistence, reminders, OCR, exports, sync, and AI control.

When does it make sense to upgrade?

Upgrade once you rely on exact URL and domain notes across sessions, or when OCR, reminders, exports, and dashboard organization save repeated effort.

Are these prices live today?

This page reflects the recommended launch pricing. Live checkout still depends on production Stripe validation and checkout URLs being wired into the extension.

Will there be a team plan?

Yes. Collaboration remains a later roadmap item, with a planned Team tier once shared collections and role controls are ready.
