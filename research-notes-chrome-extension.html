Research notes Chrome extension

Capture research notes in Chrome without separating the note from the source.

TabNotes helps researchers, students, PMs, founders, and QA reviewers keep findings attached to the page where they were discovered. Quotes, highlights, screenshots, OCR text, reminders, and follow-ups stay contextual instead of getting flattened into a generic notebook.

Typical research pain: tab overload creates a trail of half-remembered findings, screenshots, and open loops.

TabNotes fit: quick page-side capture now, searchable dashboard later, with source links still intact.

Research workflows

Useful research note tools need both capture speed and later retrieval.

Quote and highlight capture

Turn selected text into clipped notes or restored highlights so the evidence stays close to the note.

Screenshot and OCR support

Save charts, UI states, and image-based findings, then keep extracted OCR text searchable in the dashboard and side panel.

Reminder-driven follow-up

Set one-time or recurring reminders when a source needs a later check-in, pricing update, or launch review.

Shareable handoffs

Export notes or create read-only share bundles for a teammate, report, or QA review without exposing everything in the workspace.
